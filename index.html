Combo Client

Minecraft PvP client / 1.21.11

Forty-seven modules, one clean HUD, and the lyrics to whatever you've got playing.

Combo Client is a Minecraft PvP client built around a HUD you actually want on screen. Everything is a module you switch on or off, from keystrokes and CPS to a combo counter, and it shows live lyrics for your music while you play.

  • Live lyrics

    Every module in one searchable list with category filters. One click turns anything on or off.
