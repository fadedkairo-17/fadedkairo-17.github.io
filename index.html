Combo Client

Minecraft PvP client / 1.21.11

A Minecraft PvP client with 47 modules and live lyrics on your HUD.

Combo Client has many modules, themes, and quality of life changes without cheats.

Get Combo Client Join the Discord

What's in it

  • Live lyrics for the song you're playing, synced as it goes
  • 47 modules: 13 HUD, 17 PvP, 7 visual and 10 utility
  • FPS, CPS, keystrokes, ping, coordinates, direction, armor, potion effects, totem count and saturation on your HUD
  • Three text boxes you can write whatever you want in
  • A combo counter and a custom crosshair with presets
  • Themes
  • One module list with search and category tabs

About

I make stuff that runs on top of other people's games, so it has to be fast and it can't break every time the game updates.

I write C#, Java and C++, plus some Python and HTML. Most of it goes into HUDs, modules and the menus you use to set them up, made so they're easy to read while you're playing. I also make the websites for the things I build, with changelogs, status pages and setup guides.
